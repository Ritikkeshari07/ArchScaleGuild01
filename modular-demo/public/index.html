Interdependencies

Each app can connect with the other three: Contacts with Products, Orders, and Tasks; Products with Contacts, Orders, and Tasks; Orders with Contacts, Products, and Tasks; and Tasks with Contacts, Products, and Orders. Links can use an existing record or a saved text snapshot. Deleting one app's records leaves the other apps' snapshots intact.
